The Idea

Not Just a Cup. A Better Experience.

An edible cup changes what a drink feels like. Instead of finishing your drink and reaching for a bin, the cup becomes part of the moment.

  • A unique drinking experience that guests remember and talk about.
  • An alternative to conventional disposable cups for people who want to try something different.
  • Designed around a more sustainable mindset, from the first sip to the last bite.

Sustainability

Rethinking the Way We Drink.

Every day, countless drinks are served in cups that are used once and discarded. CUP2EAT starts from a simple question: what if the cup itself was part of the experience?

Our philosophy is to encourage a more thoughtful way to enjoy a drink, one that gets people talking about what they use and why.

Detailed material, composition and environmental information will be shared by CUP2EAT directly.
